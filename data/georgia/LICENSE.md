The files `snapshot.json` and `zips.json` in this folder are adapted compilations.

Source: VoteGA.org (https://votega.org), CC BY-SA 4.0.
Upstream facts: the Georgia General Assembly (https://www.legis.ga.gov/).
ZIP district crosswalk source file: https://github.com/Votega/votega.org

VoteGA's license covers their compilation, cleaning, and joins. This snapshot keeps official bill URLs, status, sponsors, roll calls, and the legislator roster, and adds plain-English summaries written for American Capitol Watch.
