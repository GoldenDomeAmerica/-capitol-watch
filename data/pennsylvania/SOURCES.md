The files `snapshot.json` and `zips.json` in this folder are a curated extract for American Capitol Watch.

Bill titles, sponsors, action history, co-sponsorship memo links, and printer’s-number PDF links come from the Pennsylvania General Assembly Bill History file for the 2025–2026 regular session:

https://www.palegis.us/data/file?documentType=BillHistoryData&session=2025_0

The House and Senate rosters (party, district, counties, phones, addresses, and photos) come from the General Assembly’s member RSS feeds:

- https://www.palegis.us/house/rss/session/members
- https://www.palegis.us/senate/rss/session/members

Floor roll calls on the shortlisted bills come from the official summary pages, for example:

https://www.palegis.us/house/roll-calls/summary?sessYr=2025&sessInd=0&rcNum=1624

ZIP-to-district matches come from the U.S. Census Bureau 2024 State Legislative District to 2020 ZCTA relationship files. A ZIP that splits across districts keeps each part. Street addresses are looked up live through the Census geocoder and are not stored here.

Credit: Pennsylvania General Assembly. The information is public and provided AS IS. It is not under a Creative Commons license.

Census relationship files are works of the U.S. Census Bureau.

Plain-English summaries in the snapshot repeat the official short title. They do not add facts. Application source code outside this data folder is not covered by the data credit above.
