# H0628 — Parent-Student On-Road Driver’s Training Program

## Liberal or opposing view (opinion)

“You don’t want to have your life as the car or your safety or your life as the person in the car next to a teenager to count on them to make split second decisions to step on the brake and how to handle that,” Berch said. “This is a safety issue.”

- Speaker: Rep. Steve Berch, Assistant Minority Leader, who voted no
- Party: Democrat, official House membership roster and Mini Directory
- Outlet: KMVT, Heatherann Wagner
- Date: February 27, 2026, 8:15 p.m. MST (10:15 p.m. ET)
- URL: https://www.kmvt.com/2026/02/28/several-measures-signed-into-law-legislative-session-continues/

## Conservative view (opinion)

A verbatim quote from a Republican supporter, better as a spoken defense than the Statement of Purpose alone:

“Current law requires six hours with a professional instructor. The other 50 are performed by the parents on these roads with their children that they know better than anybody else in society because they are their kids,” Harris said. “This is a simple bill that should be green lights across the board.”

- Speaker: Rep. Kyle Harris, who voted yes
- Party: Republican, official House membership roster and Mini Directory
- Outlet: KMVT, Heatherann Wagner
- Date: February 27, 2026, 8:15 p.m. MST (10:15 p.m. ET)

# H0656 — Aggregated student immigration and nationality data

## Liberal or opposing view (opinion)

From the ACLU of Idaho’s written position page on this bill:

“Even though the bill says data will be aggregated and not used to discriminate, the very act of collecting and categorizing students by immigration status in a public school system could create a chilling effect for undocumented or mixed-status families.”

- Speaker: ACLU of Idaho, official bill-position page, marked “Position: Oppose”
- Party: not applicable (advocacy organization, not a legislator)
