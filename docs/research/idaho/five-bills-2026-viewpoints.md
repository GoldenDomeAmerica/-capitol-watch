# H0623 — Schools, moment of silence

## Liberal or opposing view (opinion)

“No serious person in education thinks a moment of silence is going to achieve anything.”

- Speaker: Brian Coffey, president of the Nampa Education Association
- Party: not found (quoted in his role as a local education-association president, not as a legislator)
- Outlet: Idaho Education News, Sean Dolan
- Date: March 25, 2026
- URL: https://www.idahoednews.org/top-news/teachers-are-rolling-their-eyes-over-moment-of-silence-mandate/

The same article states that the Idaho Education Association was officially neutral on the legislation. No verbatim quote from a Democratic legislator who debated or voted no was found on the free pages checked. House Education Committee minutes for February 20, 2026, and Senate Education Committee minutes for March 10, 2026, record no votes from Reps. Chris Mathias and Soñia Galaviz, and from Sens. Carrie Semmelroth and Janie Ward-Engelking, but those minutes paraphrase discussion and do not place the members’ words in quotation marks. Those minutes were not used as quotes.

## Conservative view (opinion)

A verbatim quote from the Republican House floor sponsor, reported as House Education Committee testimony, that is more specific than the Statement of Purpose:

“Just that 60 seconds,” he said. “No screen time, no outside influence, just to reflect, to pray or whatever they want to do in that 60 seconds.”

- Speaker: Rep. Bruce Skaug, bill co-sponsor and House floor sponsor
- Party: Republican, official member page
- Outlet: Shoshone News-Press, Josh McDonald, reporting remarks “before the House Education Committee in February”
- Date of article: September 15, 2026 (describes February 2026 committee testimony)
- URL: https://shoshonenewspress.com/news/2026/sep/15/silver-valley-schools-settle-into-moment-of-silence-routine/

# H0559 — Internal revenue code conformity

## Liberal or opposing view (opinion)

“I just think that’s wrong,” Wintrow said of the ongoing agency cuts. “We really need to rethink that. We need to be more conservative, and in that respect, restore the revenue that we’ve taken out and cut too hard. Because our government is on the brink, and folks are suffering.”

- Speaker: Sen. Melissa Wintrow, Senate Minority Leader, who voted no
- Party: Democrat, official member page
- Outlet: Idaho Capital Sun, Laura Guido
- Date: February 6, 2026 (article timestamp 11:39 p.m. UTC, which is 6:39 p.m. ET)
- URL: https://idahocapitalsun.com/2026/02/06/idaho-senate-approves-bill-conforming-to-federal-tax-cuts-in-big-beautiful-bill/

## Conservative view (opinion)

A verbatim quote from the Republican Senate floor sponsor, better as a spoken defense than the Statement of Purpose alone:

“Even though there is a cost of $155 million projected for (fiscal year 2026), there is a huge benefit to our Idaho taxpayers.”

The parenthetical “(fiscal year 2026)” appears that way in the article.

- Speaker: Sen. Doug Ricks, bill sponsor and Senate floor sponsor
- Party: Republican, official member page
- Outlet: Idaho Capital Sun, Laura Guido
- Date: February 6, 2026
- URL: https://idahocapitalsun.com/2026/02/06/idaho-senate-approves-bill-conforming-to-federal-tax-cuts-in-big-beautiful-bill/

# H0736 — Elections, ballots

## Liberal or opposing view (opinion)

not found

This was a near-unanimous, bipartisan bill with Democratic floor sponsors. It passed the House 68-1-1 on March 5, 2026, and the Senate 34-0-1 on March 24, 2026. The House floor sponsor was Rep. Anne Henderson Haws (D); the Senate floor sponsor was Sen. James D. Ruchti (D). The only no vote was Rep. Ted Hill (R). House Democrats who voted, voted yes; Rep. Brooke Green (D) was absent. House State Affairs Committee minutes for February 26, 2026, record a do-pass recommendation after Rep. Haws presented the bill and contain no opposing testimony and no verbatim opposing quotes. No Democratic or left-leaning opposing quote was found on the free pages checked, and none is forced here.

## Conservative view (opinion)

A verbatim quote from a Republican sponsor or supporter that is better than the Statement of Purpose: not found

The Statement of Purpose contact and House floor sponsor is Rep. Anne Henderson Haws, a Democrat. The Senate floor sponsor is Sen. James D. Ruchti, a Democrat. The House State Affairs minutes summarize Haws’s presentation without placing her words, or any Republican supporter’s words, in quotation marks. No verbatim Republican-supporter quote was found in free news coverage.
